Breast Lift

A breast lift, or mastopexy, is a surgical procedure designed to lift and firm breasts that have lost their shape and firmness due to aging, weight changes, or pregnancy. This procedure can restore a youthful appearance and improve breast symmetry. During the initial consultation, your expectations are discussed, and the most suitable surgical technique is chosen to achieve the desired result.

How to Prepare

  • Have a laboratory test or a medical exam to ensure you are in good overall health.
  • Inform the surgeon about any medications you are taking and follow their guidance regarding their use.
  • Get a baseline mammogram before surgery to evaluate the condition of the breast tissue.
  • Stop smoking in advance to promote better healing.
  • Avoid taking aspirin, anti-inflammatory drugs, and herbal supplements that may increase the risk of bleeding.

What to Expect from the Procedure

  • A lift and firming of the breasts, resulting in a more youthful appearance and improved shape.
  • A recovery time that may include a few days of rest and limitation of physical activities during the first few weeks.
  • Possible swelling and discomfort in the first few days after surgery.
  • Visible results almost immediately, although the final appearance will be seen once the swelling has subsided.
  • Regular follow-ups with the surgeon to monitor recovery and address any questions or complications that may arise.

A breast lift is a safe and effective procedure to restore the firmness and position of the breasts. By choosing to undergo this surgery, you will be under the care of professionals who will guide you throughout the process, ensuring that you feel well-informed and comfortable. With proper preparation and realistic expectations, you can expect results that will enhance your confidence and well-being. If you are ready to take this important step, we are here to provide the best care and support. Regain the shape you desire and feel incredibly good about yourself!
